FEAT: SONGPOST
==============

NAME
  songpost — lyrics sync visualizer with spectrum
  rendering and self-recording video export

WHEN
  when you have an mp3 and a timestamped transcription
  and you want a music video

STEPS
  1. put the mp3 and transcription-simple.txt in the
     repo root. the transcription format is one line
     per word: "0.00 word"
  2. zig build — compiles the spectrum visualizer to
     WASM and bundles everything with bun into dist/
  3. open dist/index.html in a browser. the audio
     plays, words appear and highlight on their
     timestamps, the zig WASM spectrum paints rainbow
     arcs driven by the FFT analyser node
  4. click Export. the browser asks to share the tab.
     accept. it records itself via getDisplayMedia +
     CropTarget, capturing canvas + HTML lyrics as
     one composited video
  5. when the song ends the recorder stops and
     downloads a webm

GIVES
  a webm music video with synchronized lyrics over
  a spectrum visualization. no ffmpeg. no intermediate
  files. the browser is the compositor, the encoder,
  and the muxer.

TEST
  if the video has rainbow arcs but no words, you used
  canvas.captureStream() instead of getDisplayMedia.
  captureStream only sees canvas pixels. getDisplayMedia
  sees the whole composited page.

  if the spectrum is black, the AudioContext was not
  resumed (check autoplay policy).

  if the outline is too thick, base_line_width in
  spectrum.zig controls it. 1 is subtle. 4 is bold.

NOTE
  the canvas-as-file-descriptor thesis. the zig spectrum
  writes drawing commands as packed binary structs to fd 3.
  in the wasm build, WASI intercepts fd_write and routes
  the bytes to a typescript metapacket reader that
  deserializes and executes against CanvasRenderingContext2D.
  in the native build, fd 3 is just a file. you can pipe
  it. you can tee it. you can diff two runs with xxd.

  this is the same architecture as the reel format
  (browser renders, browser encodes) arrived at from
  the opposite direction. the reel starts from HTML.
  songpost starts from zig. both end up in the browser.

  the white outline on the spectrum arcs is controlled
  by two values in spectrum.zig:
    const base_line_width: u16 = 1;
    c.setStrokeStyle(255, 255, 255, @round(65535 * 0.2));
  lower the 0.2 to make it more subtle, raise to 0.95
  for the original bold look.

  headless chrome can record via getDisplayMedia if you
  pass these flags:
    --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required
    --use-fake-ui-for-media-stream
    --auto-select-tab-capture-source-by-url=<url>
    --enable-features=GetDisplayMediaSetAutoSelectAllScreens
